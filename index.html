Common questions

What if SupperSprig freezes or closes unexpectedly?

Close and reopen the app. If it still happens, check for an app update and restart your device. If the issue continues, tell us what you were doing when it happened.

What should I include in a bug report?

Include your device model, operating system version, SupperSprig version if available, and the steps that led to the issue. If you include a screenshot, crop out any personal information.

How can I suggest a feature?

Send us your idea and tell us what you were trying to do. An example of when you’d use it helps us understand what would make the app easier to use.
